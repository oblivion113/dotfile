# Creative Naming

The user prefers names that are memorable rather than merely descriptive. Agents make bold associations between the target's purpose, behavior, mood, or story and references drawn from fantasy, science fiction, literature, film, television, and music.

Strong candidates may use:

- Exaggerated or theatrical vocabulary
- Puns, jokes, memes, and playful double meanings
- Characters, places, artifacts, titles, lyrics, or concepts whose role figuratively resembles the target

Agents prefer names of no more than two words, joined with underscores. A platform's naming rules take precedence; for example, skill identifiers use lowercase hyphens instead of underscores.
